01 / About

Between code
and product

“I do my best work in the space between the code and the product.” — Mikael

I've spent over a decade in tech, starting in frontline IT support and growing into senior .NET consulting. I'm at home in the Microsoft stack — C#, .NET, Azure, integrations — and that technical grounding stays with me as I focus more on the product side.

That's where my focus is now: owning what gets built and why, not just how. I've run product backlogs and sprints and sat between the business and the engineers to make sure we build the right thing, not just a working thing. I want to keep moving that way, toward shaping the direction and the outcomes rather than only delivering the tasks.

My depth is in product data and digital commerce: five years building inriver's PIM platform, and now helping a large company put one into practice. That's a rare vantage point, both vendor and implementer, but it's a foundation I build on, not a box I sit in. I do my best work in the space between the code and the product.
